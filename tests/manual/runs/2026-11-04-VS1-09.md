## Open points
- The owner's preference was weak in rounds 2 and 8 ("my feeling"), and calls it the taste of now. Single hits say nothing about a kick in a loop or mix, so the new defaults are heard in a track at the CORE-01 run. → VS1-07
- No filter won a round: the filters lost at 12 dB of drive and once at 6 dB, on these settings of cutoff and env. So BODY-05's type is a proposal, not a choice by ear. → VS1-03
- A start at full level steps by design, and BODY-01c, BODY-04a and the "never clicks" rule on retriggers do not allow that today. BODY-10 sets this for a first hit; a retrigger of a ringing kick is decided there. → VS1-05
- The tone click won part of rounds 3 and 4 and lost in round 8, on one setting (3 kHz, 4 ms, -6 dB). The Click section stays as planned and is tuned there. → VS2-01
- A small difference in tuning is hardly heard in single hits, but that says nothing about a kick against a bass line. → IDEA-03
- BODY-04e measures a kick's end with the sweep off (open point of the VS1-01 run). BODY-08c measures it at the new defaults. → VS1-10
